About us

A trusted uPVC fabricator, powered by Prominance.

SGW (Sekhara Glass Works) is a Hyderabad-based uPVC fabrication company specialising in customised manufacturing and installation of premium windows and doors for residential and commercial projects.

  • Custom fabrication to your exact openings and elevation drawings
  • Genuine Prominance profiles — SKZ, BSI and IFT certified
  • Precision fitting and a professional finish, on every site
  • Transparent quotations with open test reports

SGW Prominance stands for quality fabrication, precision fitting, professional finish and customer trust.
