Inside the profile

What makes a Prominance window perform.

A cut through the frame and sash. Hover or tap a part to see what it does.

  • Glazing unitSingle, toughened or double-glazed glass with a sealed spacer.
  • EPDM gasketsSoft seals that keep out dust, rain and noise.
  • Glazing beadHolds the glass firmly in the sash.
  • Multi-chamber sashTrapped air in each chamber slows heat transfer.
  • Multi-chamber frameProminance profile, rated for wind loads up to 3000 Pa.
  • Steel reinforcementGalvanised steel inside the profile for strength.
  • Drainage slotChannels rainwater back outside.
